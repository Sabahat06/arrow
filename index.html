Arrowly – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrowly ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you play the game on Android. Arrowly is a single-player puzzle game. You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device: completed levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, streak, achievements, daily-challenge results and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, theme and arrow colours). This data is never uploaded to our servers. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads, interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to continue a level or earn coins). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, language), and ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics, crash reports and configuration (Google Firebase)

When enabled, the app uses Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics – anonymous gameplay events (for example level started, level completed, power-up used) together with an app instance ID and basic device information, so we can understand how the game is played and improve its difficulty.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports containing technical information (device model, OS version, app version and the error stack trace) so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config – downloads game settings such as prices and rewards; it does not collect personal information.

This data is not used to identify you personally. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Play services

The app may use Google Play to check for app updates and to show the standard Google Play rating prompt. These features are provided by Google and are covered by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example when a new daily challenge is available or your streak is about to end). These are created on your device; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your device's share sheet with a short message and an image of the finished puzzle. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Permissions

The app requests only: Internet and network state (to load ads, analytics and settings), vibration (haptic feedback), and – on Android 13 and later – permission to post notifications (reminders, optional). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

How information is used and shared

Information collected by the services above is used to provide and show ads, keep the game stable, understand and improve gameplay, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named in this policy (Google AdMob and Google Firebase), who process it under their own privacy policies. All data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period according to Firebase retention settings. To ask for deletion of any data associated with your app instance, contact us at the email below.

Children

Arrowly is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or limit its processing. You can exercise these rights through your device's ad settings, by uninstalling the app, or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The updated version will be available in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date.
